Enterprise Real-Time Caching Platform

High-scale financial data access

Beginning in 2020, I conceived and led the evolution of a reusable distributed caching and data-access architecture designed to reduce repeated external processing calls, lower latency and improve resilience across mission-critical financial experiences.

The platform operates at approximately 10+ billion transactions per month with peak throughput near 7,000 TPS. I provide architectural leadership across a global multidisciplinary organization of more than 50 professionals.

Architecture Capabilities

Distributed caching: low-latency access for high-throughput financial workloads.

Cache warming: pre-positioning frequently requested data to reduce downstream dependency.

Consistency and invalidation: maintaining freshness as authoritative data changes.

Replication and resilience: synchronization across distributed runtime foundations.

Hybrid deployment: architecture spanning private/on-premises and public-cloud environments.

Enterprise Context

Synchrony publicly reports approximately 70.7 million active accounts, and its 2025 fact sheet describes more than 100 million consumer credit accounts. Synchrony has also publicly described a broader modernization strategy involving hybrid cloud, enterprise data platforms, APIs and AI.

Business and Data Impact

The architecture has supported low-latency access to frequently requested data, large-scale historical statement access and substantial annual cost avoidance by reducing repeated chargeable external calls. Proprietary implementation details, internal identifiers and confidential topology are intentionally not published.

Research Extension: Agentic Caching

Production lessons from large-scale caching systems informed my research into multi-agent approaches for predictive, self-optimizing in-memory middleware.
