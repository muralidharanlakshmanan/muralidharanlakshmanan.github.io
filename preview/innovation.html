Enterprise Real-Time Caching & Data Platform

High-scale financial data access

Beginning in 2020, I conceived and led the evolution of a reusable distributed caching and data-access architecture designed to reduce repeated external processing calls, lower latency and improve resilience across mission-critical financial experiences.

The platform operates at approximately 10+ billion transactions per month with peak throughput near 7,000 TPS. I provide architectural leadership across a global multidisciplinary organization of more than 50 professionals.

Official CIS 2026 speaker profile ↗

Architecture Capabilities

Distributed caching: low-latency access patterns for high-throughput workloads.

Cache warming: pre-positioning frequently requested data to reduce downstream dependency.

Consistency and invalidation: keeping cached data appropriately synchronized with authoritative systems.

Replication and resilience: synchronization across distributed runtime foundations.

Hybrid deployment: operation across private/on-premises and public-cloud environments.

Enterprise Context

Synchrony publicly reports approximately 70.7 million active accounts and has separately documented more than 100 million consumer credit accounts. The company has also described a technology transformation involving hybrid cloud, enterprise data platforms, APIs and AI.

Business and Data Impact

At a public-portfolio level, the architecture has supported low-latency access to frequently requested data, historical statement access at large data volumes, and substantial annual cost avoidance by reducing repeated chargeable external calls.
