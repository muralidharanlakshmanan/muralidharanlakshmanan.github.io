About Me

I am Muralidharan Lakshmanan, a technology leader and enterprise architect with more than 17 years of experience designing and modernizing large-scale systems across financial services, healthcare and supply chain.

I currently work as a VP, Solutions Architect, where my responsibilities span distributed systems, caching, data platforms, cloud architecture, API strategy and AI-enabled enterprise systems.

My work includes architectural leadership for a real-time caching ecosystem processing approximately 10 billion transactions per month with peak throughput near 7,000 TPS, and leadership across global multidisciplinary teams of more than 50 professionals.

Alongside industry work, I publish research, write for practitioner audiences, speak at technical conferences, review research papers and serve on technical program committees.
