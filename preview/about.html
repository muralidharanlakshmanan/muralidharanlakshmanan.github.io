About Me

I am an engineering leader, enterprise architect and researcher with more than 17 years of experience building and modernizing large-scale systems across financial services, healthcare and supply chain. My work combines architecture strategy, hands-on system design, platform modernization, engineering leadership and the translation of complex business requirements into reliable technology solutions.

In my current role as VP, Solutions Architect at Synchrony, I define platform roadmaps and lead architecture across high-volume APIs, microservices, caching and data platforms. My work includes a real-time caching middleware ecosystem processing 10B+ API data requests per month at ~20 ms average response time and ~7,000 requests per second at peak, as well as high-volume data platforms supporting analytics, reporting and reconciliation. I lead and mentor a global multidisciplinary organization of more than 50 professionals and serve as a technical advisor across product, business, engineering, client and vendor teams.

My background also includes large-scale cloud and middleware modernization. I have led migrations of more than 300 middleware packages and web services from legacy Solaris environments to converged cloud and PCF platforms, designed enterprise integration services processing approximately 150 million transactions per day, and delivered high-performance caching and data capabilities that generated $12-15 million in annual cost avoidance.

Alongside my industry career, I actively contribute to the broader technology community through research, invited speaking, practitioner writing, peer review and technical program committee service. My current areas of interest include Agentic AI, large language models, RAG, intelligent caching, multi-agent orchestration and resilient AI-native enterprise architecture.
