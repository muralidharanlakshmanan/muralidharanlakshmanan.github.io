About Me

I am Muralidharan Lakshmanan, a VP, Solutions Architect with more than 17 years of professional experience designing and modernizing large-scale enterprise systems across financial services, healthcare and supply chain.

My technical focus includes distributed systems, cloud-native architecture, APIs, caching, data platforms, event-driven systems and applied AI. I have led architecture and engineering work for mission-critical platforms operating at very high transaction volumes and have guided global multidisciplinary teams of more than 50 professionals.

Alongside industry work, I am an active researcher, invited speaker, reviewer and technical committee contributor. My current research interests include Agentic AI, multi-agent orchestration, intelligent caching and resilient AI-native enterprise architecture.
