BIOGRAPHY

Architecture, research and technical leadership.

I have spent my career working on systems where reliability and scale are part of the business model, not just technical qualities. I began in enterprise middleware and healthcare EDI, expanded into supply-chain integration and cloud modernization, and later moved into architecture leadership for high-volume financial platforms.

At Synchrony, I provide architecture and engineering leadership across distributed systems, caching, data platforms, cloud modernization and AI-enabled systems. My work includes a caching middleware ecosystem processing approximately 10 billion transactions per month with peak throughput near 7,000 TPS, along with leadership of global multidisciplinary teams of more than 50 professionals.

Alongside industry work, I publish research and practitioner articles, speak at technical conferences, review research papers and conference proposals, and serve on technical program committees. My current research interests include Agentic AI, intelligent caching, multi-agent orchestration and resilient AI-native architecture.
