01 THE PROBLEM

Before 2020, frequently requested customer and account data was retrieved through external third-party calls. Each call carried a transaction cost and added latency across customer- and client-facing channels, at the scale of Synchrony's 70.7M active accounts and 100M+ consumer credit accounts.

02 WHAT I BUILT

No equivalent capability existed in the organization. I conceived and led a new, reusable distributed caching and real-time data architecture, not a reimplementation of an existing system, and have owned its design and evolution since 2020. I lead the 50+ person global team that builds and operates it.

03 ARCHITECTURE CAPABILITIES
cache.distributed()

Distributed caching & event streaming

GemFire-based distributed caching with Kafka event streaming for high-throughput data movement.

cache.warm()

Cache warming

Pre-positions frequently requested data so common access paths are ready before demand arrives.

consistency.control()

Invalidation & consistency

Controls aligned to the source of record to maintain appropriate data freshness.

foundation.replicate()

Cross-foundation replication

Supports resilience and horizontal scale across distributed runtime foundations.

consumer.configure()

Reusable configuration

Configurable per consuming application or client without redesigning the underlying platform.

deploy.hybrid()

Hybrid deployment

Supports on-premises, private cloud and public cloud deployment models.

statements.history()

Historical-statement extension

100+ TB historical statement data, 12-18 months history, with sub-100 ms access.

04 BUSINESS IMPACT
  • 10B+ API data requests served per month at ~20 ms average response time.
  • $12-15 million annual cost avoidance from reduced external calls, changing how the business funds and operates its digital infrastructure.
  • Underpins web, mobile, customer-service and virtual-assistant experiences.
  • Powers analytics for customers, external clients and internal teams.
  • No re-platforming required across six years of growth (2020-2026).
