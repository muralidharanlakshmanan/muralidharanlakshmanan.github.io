Real-Time Data & Caching Architecture

The Challenge

High-volume digital experiences repeatedly needed customer and account information from authoritative downstream systems. At scale, that pattern introduced latency, transaction cost and architectural dependency.

The objective was not simply to add a cache. I conceived a reusable enterprise data-access architecture that could serve eligible requests internally while preserving freshness, consistency, resilience and source-of-truth ownership.

What I Architected
Distributed cachingLow-latency data access at financial-services scale.
Cache warmingPre-positioning frequently requested data.
Consistency & invalidationPatterns for freshness as source data changes.
Replication & resilienceSynchronization across multiple runtime foundations.
Hybrid deploymentArchitecture spanning private/on-prem and public-cloud environments.
